About Us

Precision wiring, honest work, built on Voltage Pro Electrical's name.

We started this company on a simple premise: electrical work in high-end homes and commercial spaces deserves the same level of craftsmanship as the finishes around it. No shortcuts, no exposed panels behind cabinetry, no guesswork on load calculations. Every job — from a single panel upgrade to a full estate rewire — gets the same careful attention from our licensed journeymen and master electricians.

We're fully licensed, bonded, and insured in every jurisdiction we serve, and every crew member on site is a background-checked employee — never a subcontractor passing through.
